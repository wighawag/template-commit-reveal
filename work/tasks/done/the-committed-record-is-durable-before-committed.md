# A tab closed right after a commit could keep only the pre-commit record

Found by stratagems' step 8 (local `step-8`, `work/notes/observations/the-committed-turn-is-written-durably-only-before-the-commit.md`, and the plan's log entry "2026-10-30, step 8: the race fix merged, and the e2e still fails"). Its `e2e/tests/stratagems-fuzd.e2e.ts` commits, waits for the HUD to say "Scheduled: this turn will be revealed for you, even if you close this page.", and closes the tab. In about one full run in three, `localStorage` just before the close held `"committed":false` and no committed turn, and the next page, in a later cycle, dropped the record as an uncommitted plan and showed `Idle`, though fuzd had revealed the turn. Without a scheduler the same gap meant a missed reveal could not be settled with its chunks (no `committedTurns` entry), so the WHOLE bond was forfeited instead of one gem a move. The code is item 4's (`the-submission-in-the-account-data.md`) and the race fix's (`a-restored-turn-keeps-its-record-until-the-chain-answers.md`), in `web/src/lib/game/core/submission.ts`, byte-identical in every descendant.

**The mechanism.** `commit()` wrote the pre-commit record durably (`saveDurably`, `committed: false`, the secret and the hash) before sending, as the file comment requires. Once the commitment was on chain it wrote the committed record (`committed: true`, `revealScheduled`, the layers' `extensions`; in `commit-reveal/storage.ts`'s account-data storage also the `committedTurns` entry, written once) with a plain `storage.save`, which goes through synqable's 100 ms local-storage debounce and an asynchronous save, and set `Committed` at once.

## Done, 2026-10-30, on local preview branches, NOT pushed (awaiting the maintainer's approval)

On `main` (preview branch `fix/committed-record-durable` from `origin/main` e92f4a21, worktree `~/dev/worktrees/template-commit-reveal/fix/committed-record-durable`), test-first, one commit:

1. `ee68c542` The committed record goes through `saveDurably` where the storage has it (`storeCommitted`), and `Committed` is reported only once it is on storage; the generation is checked after that await, as after every other one. A durable write that FAILS after the commitment is on chain undoes nothing: the turn is `Committed` with a new optional `notStored: {message, error}` on the state, nothing is cleared, nothing is thrown, and this page still reveals it (the secret was durable before the send, and in the account data the record is still in memory for a later save). A `reveal()` asked for while the commit is still recording its turn returns: storage may already hold the committed record (memory is written at once), but the commit owns the turn until it has reported it and would overwrite a reveal's state with `Committed`; the cycle reveals it once it is `Committed`. A storage without `saveDurably` keeps the plain save. The `saveDurably` port comment says it is awaited twice now.

**Which writes became durable, and which did not.** Every write of a committed record or its `extensions` was checked:

- `commit()`'s post-commit record: made durable (this fix). It is the one written while the HUD tells the player the turn is safe to leave.
- The scheduler's path: nothing separate to change. `with/fuzd`'s scheduler writes no storage of its own; its `extensions` (`{fuzd: FuzdSchedule}`) come back through the adapter's commit outcome into the same record, so they are durable with it.
- `take()`'s save (an `adopt()` from recovery, or a plan the chain's hash shows was committed): left a plain `save`. It is synchronous by API (`adopt` returns a boolean), and what it writes is a reconstruction of something already recoverable: the chain holds the hash, and the secret is either in the durable pre-commit record or re-derivable by the recovery that produced it.

**Tests** (9 new). `web/test/lib/game/core/submission.test.ts`, a new `describe` over a storage with a MEMORY and a DISK whose every durable write the test holds, releases or fails (5): `Committed` not reported while the committed record's write is held, the disk still at `committed: false`, the held write carrying `revealScheduled` and `extensions`; a reload from the disk once `Committed` is reported restores `Committed` (scheduled) in the same cycle and, in a later cycle, closes it as an earlier committed turn (`Missed` with this adapter) rather than dropping it (`Idle`, the e2e's symptom); a failed post-commit write leaves `Committed` with `notStored`, the commit resolving, memory committed, and the turn still revealed; no reveal sent while the write is held; a storage without `saveDurably` saves twice and reports as before. `web/test/lib/game/core/submission-account-data.test.ts`, over the real account data and the harness's controlled storage (4): with saves held after the commitment lands, still `Committing`, and once released, the plan slot and the `committedTurns` entry on storage the moment `Committed` is reported, with no `persisted()` awaited; a page opened in a later cycle with nothing awaited after `Committed` finds the committed turn (`Missed`, `committedTurns` kept); a save error after the commitment is reported as `notStored` (`quota exceeded`), the turn kept; an account switch during the held write leaves the next account `Idle` and writes nothing into its storage, the turn reaching ALICE's. **Red first** on `origin/main`: 7 failed (`Committed` where `Committing` was expected, twice; the reload showing `Planning` and, in the account data, `Idle`; no second durable write to fail and no `notStored`; a reveal sent), 2 green (the account switch and the plain storage, guards). **Green after the fix.** Mutation-checked: removing the reveal guard turns its test red.

## Not done here

- Nothing pushed: the maintainer approves each push.
- stratagems' integration branch and `step-8` are not touched: its between-steps merge, step 8's e2e measured again over several full runs, and its landing are the stratagems session's. The game e2e suites of reveal-or-die, bomber-world and stratagems were not run (the change is in `game/core` only, covered by the template's and `with/fuzd`'s e2e and the unit tests).
- The failing e2e is not reproduced here: it is stratagems'. The template's closed-page fuzd test (`fuzd.e2e.ts`) passed after the change as before; the deterministic reproduction is the unit tests.
- **A window this fix does not close, not verified in a test:** a tab closed while the commit is still `Committing` (the wallet prompt, or the wait for the receipt, before the committed record is written at all) leaves only the pre-commit record, `committed: false` with its `commitmentHash`. In the same cycle the chain's hash takes it back up (`storedTurnFor`); in a LATER cycle `restore()` clears an uncommitted record of an earlier cycle as a plan without asking the chain, so the same full-bond loss is possible there. The HUD does not invite a close in that window, unlike after `Committed`. Asking the chain (`commitmentStatus`, or the missed-reveal reader) before clearing a record that carries a `commitmentHash` would close it; that is a decision for a task of its own.
- The `notStored` report is on the state only: no HUD in the tree shows it yet.
