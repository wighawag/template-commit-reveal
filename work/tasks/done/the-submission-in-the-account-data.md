2. `b7f90c09` The scheduler seam (P3). `RevealScheduler<TIdentity, TExtensions>` (seams.ts) is a part (`parts.scheduler`) handed to the adapter's `commit`. `prepare(request)` runs before the commit and returns `{payee, value}`, which `makeCommitment` carries (`zeroAddress` and no value without a scheduler). `submit(request & {commitTransaction, payment})` runs once the commit is BROADCAST, beside the wait for its receipt, and may return the layer's `Partial<TExtensions>`. `request` is `{identity, cycleNumber, revealDueAt, transactions}`, `transactions` the reveal as `RevealTransaction[]` (`{to, data, gas}`, one per chunk, in order, payee zero), also exposed as `adapter.revealTransactions` and `buildRevealTransactions`. Either half failing leaves the commit standing and reports `revealScheduled: false`; no `revealDueAt` (a manual chain) asks nothing. The submission records `revealScheduled` on the `Committed` state and the persisted turn, reads anything but `true` from an adapter handed a scheduler as not scheduled, and reveals such a turn `immediately` under `autoReveal: 'fallback'` (P7). `PersistedSubmission<TAction, TExtensions extends object = {}>` (with `extensions?: TExtensions`) and `GameTypes`' optional `extensions` arrive here, because `submit` is their first writer.
3. `29a1f978` A reveal by somebody else is `Revealed` (P9). `adapter.commitmentStatus({identity, actions, secret, cycleNumber}): Promise<'open' | 'revealed' | 'unknown'>` (one `getCommitment`: held for the cycle is `open`; cycle zero with the head at this turn's LAST chunk is `revealed`); `adapter.reveal` returns `RevealOutcome` (`{hash} | {alreadyRevealed: true}`) instead of throwing when it finds the turn opened. The submission asks the chain at the cycle turn, in `restore()` and on a late reveal before calling a turn `Missed`, treats a failed reveal of a turn the chain reports revealed as `Revealed`, and runs `onSettled` in every `Revealed` path. An adapter without `commitmentStatus` keeps the old reading.
4. `66824022` Readiness (Q8-1, Q8-6). `SubmissionStorage` stays synchronous and gains `ready?: Readable<boolean>` (absent: always ready) and `saveDurably?(record): Promise<void>`. The commit stores its secret with `saveDurably` before sending, and a rejection refuses it (`Error`, `during: 'commit'`). ## Not done here, and why

- **reveal-or-die's and bomber-world's `e2e/tests/recover-submission.e2e.ts`** destroyed the submission by deleting `__world_submission_*` keys, which no longer exist (it failed at "there was a stored submission to destroy"). Fixed with the maintainer's approval in reveal-or-die (`38107b60`: `storedSubmissionRecords` and `forgetSubmissionAndReload` in `e2e/fixtures/board.ts`), carried to bomber-world by its merge; both full e2e suites pass.
- `settled(cycle)` prunes every committed turn at or before `cycle` beyond the latest three, which could include an older MISSED turn not yet settled; the reference game is protected because a missed reveal blocks committing. Pruning only the settled cycle is the safer rule if a game lets play continue past an unsettled miss (a review note, not changed here).
- Other games' HUDs (reveal-or-die, bomber-world) get the blocking (`readyToPlay`) but not the "saved turn is loading" line: their HUD is theirs. `game.savedTurn` is there for them.
- stratagems' `submission-storage.ts` and `context.ts` (integration branch) must move onto `createAccountDataSubmissionStorage` at the stratagems session's next merge; their imports of `createSubmissionStorage` and `loadCommittedTurn` no longer compile.

## Decisions

- The two fields are added in the TEMPLATE's `context/core.ts`, not through a jolly-roger seam for app fields: `createAccountData({fields})` exists at jolly-roger but nothing composes an app's fields into the core's account data, and a `$app-specific` export would have been a required edit to every game's `game.ts`. Every app on this template is a commit-reveal game, so the framework's own fields belong to the template. A jolly-roger `createCoreContext` seam for app fields remains the general answer if an app ever needs fields of its own.
- The account data's records type `extensions` as `Readonly<Record<string, unknown>>` (JSON); the game gets them back as its composed `TExtensions` through `SubmissionStorage<TAction, TExtensions>`, the one cast being inside the framework's decoder.
- Committed turns are keyed by the commitment hash too, so two devices' turns for one cycle never share a key; the chain's live commitment decides between them.
- The schedule is submitted once the commit is broadcast, BESIDE its receipt wait (not after it): the tab is likeliest to close right after a commit.
- `alreadyRevealed` is read from the head: cycle zero with the head at this turn's last chunk. A cancellation of a one-chunk turn and a settlement shown the chunks look the same; both need the player's own other device, and the missed-reveal reader still asks the chain whether anything is owed.
